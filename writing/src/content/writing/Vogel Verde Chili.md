*Pork and green chile stewed with tomatoes and lemon. From Dan Vogel, December 2002.*

## Ingredients

**Chilies**

- 16–20 Big John chilies from Musso Farms, Pueblo, CO, roasted
- Black nitrile or latex gloves

**Base**

- 1½ lb pork, cubed
- 1–2 onions, diced
- Garlic, minced
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 2–3 cups broth: water + 1 Tbsp beef bouillon
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 2 lb tomatoes
- 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp cumin
- Salt & pepper to taste

## Instructions

### Prep the chilies

Wear gloves.

1. Run the chilies under warm water.
2. Pick off and rinse off the hard burnt skin.
3. Remove the stems.
4. Wash out all the seeds.
5. Dice.

### Cook

1. Dice the onions and garlic and put them in the bottom of the pot with the olive oil to simmer.
2. While they simmer, cube the pork.
3. After 10–15 minutes, add the pork and let it simmer, stirring occasionally.
4. While the pork simmers, wash, seed, and dice the chilies.
5. Toss the chilies in and pour in the broth.
6. Add the lemon juice and simmer about 2 hours.
7. Add the tomatoes and Worcestershire. Salt & pepper to taste.
8. Finish with the cumin.

## Notes

### Large batch variation

- 3 Tbsp olive oil
- 4 Tbsp butter
- 3 Tbsp chopped garlic
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- 2½–3 lb cubed pork shoulder roast
- 1 lemon, juiced

1. Cook the onions and garlic in olive oil and butter until done. Add half the lemon juice to tame the strength of the onions and garlic.
2. Stir in the pork and simmer, adding the second half of the lemon juice. Cook in the juice 1 hour.

### Marinade variation

Marinade (mix in a saucepan):

- 1 can Campbell's beef consommé
- 1 beef bouillon cube
- 1 spicy chicken bouillon cube
- 2 cups water
- ½ cup soy sauce or Kikkoman teriyaki
- 2 Tbsp liquid smoke

1. Add marinade to the mix. Simmer 3 hours with the juice of 1 lime.
2. Remove meat and oil from the pan into a skillet. Brown the meat, adding the juice of 2 limes as it browns. Add back to the mix, bring to a light boil, and simmer.
3. Thicken with ¼ cup each milk & flour. Eat with tortillas.
